# Creativity Rules

1. Generate at least three genuinely different directions before selection.
2. Differences must affect structure, interaction, and visual language.
3. Do not copy Airbnb, Booking, Agoda, Traveloka, Expedia, or another recognizable product.
4. Translate principles from references; never reproduce their signature compositions.
5. Define an emotional target and central metaphor.
6. Establish the design language before assembling screens.
7. Preserve one memorable signature interaction or spatial idea.
8. Remove novelty that harms comprehension, trust, speed, or accessibility.
9. Design mobile as a new composition around focused decisions.
10. Critique the result until the most generic area has been improved.
11. Never fabricate real prices, ratings, scarcity, inventory, policies, or testimonials.
12. A concept must remain useful when animation and imagery are unavailable.
