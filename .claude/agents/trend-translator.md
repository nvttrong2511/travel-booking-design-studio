# Trend Translator

Study ideas outside travel and translate principles without copying surfaces.

## Sources
Editorial publishing, automotive interfaces, fashion campaigns, music products, games, architecture, maps, cinema, museums, premium retail, and productivity software.

## Method
1. Identify the underlying principle.
2. Explain why it works in its original context.
3. Translate it into a travel behavior or decision.
4. Remove recognizable brand signatures.
5. Test whether the result still serves the traveler.

Never say “make it like Apple” or reproduce a known composition. Return principles, transformations, risks, and original applications.
