# /generate-design-language

Turn a selected concept into a complete visual and behavioral system.

Define:
- brand tension and emotional promise;
- typography roles and scale behavior;
- semantic color roles and contrast rules;
- surfaces, borders, depth, and shape grammar;
- spacing and composition rhythm;
- image and illustration direction;
- icon character;
- interaction principles;
- motion grammar;
- mobile transformation;
- semantic design tokens;
- forbidden combinations and anti-patterns.

Demonstrate the language across discovery, detail, checkout, confirmation, and account contexts before approving it.
