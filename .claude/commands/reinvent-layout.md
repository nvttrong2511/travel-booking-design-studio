# /reinvent-layout

Rebuild the information architecture and spatial model without changing the product goal.

1. Map required content, actions, and decision points.
2. Identify the current page formula and why it feels generic.
3. Produce three alternate structures with different reading paths.
4. Include one conservative, one bold, and one experimental direction.
5. Define desktop, tablet, and mobile transformation for each.
6. Check long content, localization, loading, errors, and unavailable inventory.
7. Recommend the strongest structure and explain what it enables that the original could not.
