# /challenge-design

Stress-test an existing design or concept.

1. Identify its central idea in one sentence.
2. List the five most generic decisions.
3. Detect resemblance to travel marketplaces, SaaS templates, and trend clichés.
4. Separate structural weakness from visual weakness.
5. Challenge the layout, discovery model, interaction, emotional tone, and mobile behavior.
6. Produce three escalation options: restrained, bold, and radical.
7. State what must remain unchanged for usability and trust.

End with a clear verdict and revision priorities.
