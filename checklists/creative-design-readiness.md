# Creative Design Readiness

## Concept
- [ ] At least three structurally different concepts were explored.
- [ ] The selected direction has a clear thesis and emotional target.
- [ ] The central metaphor affects structure or behavior, not only decoration.
- [ ] Resemblance to known travel marketplaces and SaaS templates was reviewed.
- [ ] The strongest advantage and main risk are documented.

## System
- [ ] Typography, color roles, surfaces, shape, spacing, imagery, icons, and motion form one language.
- [ ] Signature components share a reusable grammar.
- [ ] Mobile is intentionally recomposed.
- [ ] Loading, empty, error, partial, unavailable, disabled, and success states are defined.

## Experience
- [ ] Price, dates, availability, policies, and primary actions remain easy to find.
- [ ] Interactions include feedback, cancellation, keyboard, touch, latency, and failure behavior.
- [ ] Motion has a purpose and a reduced-motion alternative.
- [ ] The experience remains usable without animation and rich imagery.

## Trust and accessibility
- [ ] No fake urgency, scarcity, ratings, inventory, prices, or policies are presented as real.
- [ ] Semantic structure and focus order are correct.
- [ ] Contrast, text scaling, touch targets, errors, and non-color communication are covered.
- [ ] Signature interactions have accessible equivalents.

## Delivery
- [ ] The implementation preserves the concept thesis.
- [ ] Performance and technical risk are acceptable.
- [ ] The Design Review Board has no score below 3.
- [ ] Originality, booking clarity, accessibility, and trust each score at least 4.
