# Originality with Utility

Original design is not unfamiliarity for its own sake. It is a clear product idea expressed through structure, behavior, language, and rhythm.

## Four tests

### Structural originality
The experience organizes content and decisions in a way that fits its specific travel promise.

### Behavioral originality
The interaction model helps users explore, compare, understand, or commit more naturally.

### Emotional originality
The tone creates a precise feeling rather than generic excitement or luxury.

### Visual originality
Typography, surfaces, imagery, shape, and motion form one recognizable system.

## Utility boundary
A bold choice fails when it hides price or policy information, increases comparison effort, breaks keyboard or touch use, depends on motion, creates false urgency, or cannot handle real data.

The goal is not maximum novelty. The goal is a memorable idea that makes the travel decision easier and more meaningful.
